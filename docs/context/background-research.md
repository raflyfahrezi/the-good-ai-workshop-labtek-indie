# Background Research

## Most useful find: ai.bandungbergerak.id

Bandung Bergerak runs the program's public microsite, **"AI, Jurnalisme & Kepentingan Publik"** at https://ai.bandungbergerak.id (sub-project P002).

**Bandung Bergerak's own AI guidelines (agreed July 2026)**, https://ai.bandungbergerak.id/panduan-ai:
- Allowed: translation, transcription, document analysis, reading maps
- Not allowed: AI producing final products (text, images, video)
- **Never put confidential info, source identities, unpublished documents or personal data into AI**
- A human checks every AI output before publication
- AI use disclosed on all platforms
- Cites the Dewan Pers code of ethics and AJI/AMSI guidance

This is the likely output style of P009 (AI Code of Conduct). Any prototype that respects these rules will feel "right" to the room.

**Journalism AI Hackathon**, https://ai.bandungbergerak.id/artikel/semua-hal-tentang-journalism-ai-hackathon (sub-project P008):
- By the Bandung Bergerak + Labtek Indie + icebauhaus consortium, "Good AI for Journalism", **part of the Research Exhibition**
- Theme "Exploring AI for Better Environmental Journalism", tracks **Tanah, Air, Udara**
- Online pre-hackathon: 30 Oct, 3 Nov, 5 Nov. **Registration closes 7 Nov**
- In person **15-17 Nov 2026 at Fragment Project**, 12 teams of 2-3, coding not required, prizes Rp 10 juta across two categories
- Mentors/judges: Baban Gandapurnama (Detik), Titik Kartitiani (Pulitzer Center rainforest grantee, chair of FOKSI), Abie Besman (Unpad AI & Big Data Center)

## Journocoders Indonesia (my Session 1 crowd)

- Jakarta community where journalists and data enthusiasts learn scraping, analysis and visualisation. Started **March 2018** as a branch of Journocoders London. Mostly free monthly workshops, often online Saturdays. Meetup: ~1,900 members, 53 past events. https://www.meetup.com/journocoders-in-indonesia/
- Lead organiser: **Aghnia Adzkia** (data journalism pioneer, listed as Asia Pacific visual/data journalist at BBC News), plus four co-organisers
- Members: journalists, activists, programmers, academics, students. Held Indonesia's first Data and Computational Journalism Conference (2022)
- Recent topics: **forest-fire hotspots and haze mapping**, OSINT, politicians' conflicts of interest, online gambling and scam centres. Tools: R, QGIS, scraping KPU data
- X @journocoders_id, GitHub https://github.com/journocoders-id
- **Implication:** these journalists already scrape and code. Expect sharper technical questions than a typical newsroom. Ideas will likely lean to pain point 1 (information discovery). It's fine to go one level more technical with them, while keeping the presentation plain for the facilitators and the final report
- IDJN (Indonesian Data Journalism Network) is a separate group

## Regulation and industry context (Indonesia)

- **Peraturan Dewan Pers No. 1/PERATURAN-DP/I/2025** on AI in journalistic work, launched 24 Jan 2025 (8 chapters, 10 articles). Requires human control from start to finish, compliance with KEJ (code of ethics), verification of AI-generated data/images/audio/video, copyright respect, consent for AI likenesses and voice clones, **disclosure of AI use**, privacy and human rights, and press companies take responsibility for AI output (disputes through Dewan Pers). https://www.hukumonline.com/pusatdata/detail/lt67a9a0136fe9f/peraturan-dewan-pers-nomor-1-peraturan-dp-i-2025-tahun-2025/analysis/
- **AMSI**: Indonesia Digital Conference 2025 (22-23 Oct) themed "Sovereign AI". Reported ~30% of traffic to member sites comes from AI crawler bots. Planned to license member content to AI platforms via AMSInews.id by Q3 2026 [unverified whether it happened]. https://tirto.id/idc-2025-amsi-ada-ancaman-ai-terhadap-eksistensi-media-hkeF
- **AJI**: 2024 report on AI in Indonesian newsrooms (job losses, bias). Co-hosted CTRL+J APAC 2025 with AMSI. No single formal AJI AI position paper found
- **Perpres 32/2024 "Publisher Rights"**: signed 20 Feb 2024. Obliges digital platforms to support quality journalism. Committee KTP2JB, guidelines released Mar 2025. Relevant to pain point 6 (value capture)
- **UU PDP 27/2022** (personal data protection), fully applicable since Oct 2024, **no explicit press exemption**. A Constitutional Court challenge by AJI, SAFEnet and others (No. 135/PUU-XXIII/2025) was reportedly rejected on 19 Jan 2026 [check mkri.id]. Same day, case 145/PUU-XXIII/2025 on the Press Law was partly granted (press disputes go through Dewan Pers first)
- **PP 33/2026** (PDP implementing regulation): promulgated 16 Jul 2026, **effective 16 Jan 2027**. Lists **AI and machine learning as triggers for a DPIA**, fines up to 2% of annual revenue. https://siplawfirm.id/resources/pp-33-2026-pelindungan-data-pribadi
- **UU ITE** (second amendment, UU 1/2024): still the defamation/disinformation risk for AI-generated content (background knowledge, not re-checked)

**Why this matters in the room:** "Local AI for data privacy" isn't just nice to have. Bandung Bergerak's own rules forbid putting source identities into AI tools, and from Jan 2027 using AI on personal data can trigger a DPIA under PP 33/2026. A local redaction gate is the cheapest way to stay inside both.
